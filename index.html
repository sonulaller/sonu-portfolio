About Me

Who is Sonu Laller?

I'm Sonu, from a small village called Kasan in District Kaithal, Haryana. I never went through a formal coding course — I picked up web development on my own, one problem at a time, by actually building something real: a complete website for New Little Star Public School, from the ground up.

From setting up the frontend and backend, to wiring in an AI chatbot, fixing deployment issues, and running a full SEO pass — I did all of it myself, learning as I went. I don't wait to "know enough" before starting. I start, and I figure it out.

Alongside development, I also run an Instagram page where I create AI-generated video content — exploring how automation and new AI tools can speed up creative work.

📍 Kasan · Kaithal

My Story

I'm not from a technical background, and nobody around me was building websites or working with AI tools. But I didn't let that stop me — I taught myself by doing, breaking things, and fixing them again.

Shipping the NLSP School website taught me more than any course could — real servers, real errors, real deployment, real users. That hands-on grind is what I trust the most.

I believe in building things that actually work for the people using them — not just projects that look good in a portfolio.
